SUPPLY AGREEMENT between Vijay Textiles and Nandini Boutique, signed 1 April 2026.

1. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay every invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

2. Late Payment
Overdue amounts carry interest of 1.5% per month, starting fifteen (15) days after the due date.

3. Early Payment
No discount is offered for early payment.

4. Returns and Disputes
Defective fabric must be reported in writing within seven (7) days of delivery.

5. Termination
Either party may end this agreement with forty-five (45) days' written notice.
