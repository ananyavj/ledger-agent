# Supply Agreement: Meera Packaging Solutions and Mehta Textiles

This Supply Agreement is made on 16 November 2025 between Meera Packaging Solutions, Bhosari, Pune ("Supplier") and Mehta Textiles ("Buyer").

## 1. Scope of supply
The Supplier will supply corrugated boxes, printed cartons and related packaging materials against purchase orders issued by the Buyer from time to time.

## 2. Pricing
Prices are as per the quotation accepted in each purchase order and are exclusive of GST, which shall be charged at the applicable rate.

## 3. Payment terms
All invoices are payable no later than thirty (30) calendar days after they are issued.

## 4. Late payment
No interest or late payment charges shall apply to delayed payments. However, the Supplier may suspend further deliveries if any invoice remains unpaid for more than sixty (60) days after its due date.

## 5. Early payment
If the Buyer pays an invoice in full within seven (7) days of the invoice date, the Buyer may deduct a prompt payment discount of 1.0% of the invoice value.

## 6. Disputes
Any complaint about the quantity or quality of goods must be raised in writing within ten (10) days of delivery. Amounts that are not under dispute must still be paid by the due date.

## 7. Termination
Either party may end this agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice. Invoices raised before termination remain payable on their original terms.

## 8. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
