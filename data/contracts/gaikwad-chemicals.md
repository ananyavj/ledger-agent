# Supply Agreement: Meera Packaging Solutions and Gaikwad Chemicals

This Supply Agreement is made on 21 March 2025 between Meera Packaging Solutions, Bhosari, Pune ("Supplier") and Gaikwad Chemicals ("Buyer").

## 1. Scope of supply
The Supplier will supply corrugated boxes, printed cartons and related packaging materials against purchase orders issued by the Buyer from time to time.

## 2. Pricing
Prices are as per the quotation accepted in each purchase order and are exclusive of GST, which shall be charged at the applicable rate.

## 3. Payment terms
All invoices are payable no later than forty-five (45) calendar days after they are issued.

## 4. Late payment
Overdue invoices will be charged a late payment fee of 2.0 percent per month on the outstanding balance. A grace period of seven (7) days after the due date shall apply before any such charge is levied.

## 5. Early payment
No discount is offered for early payment.

## 6. Disputes
Any complaint about the quantity or quality of goods must be raised in writing within seven (7) days of delivery. Amounts that are not under dispute must still be paid by the due date.

## 7. Termination
Either party may end this agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice. Invoices raised before termination remain payable on their original terms.

## 8. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
